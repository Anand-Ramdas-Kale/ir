\section{Performance Metrics}

Total parallel overhead
$$
T_o = pT_p-T_s
$$

Speedup
\begin{align*}
S&=\frac{T_s}{T_p} \\
S &\le p
\end{align*}

Efficiency
\begin{align*}
  E&=\frac{S}{p} \\
  1&\le E\le p
\end{align*}
